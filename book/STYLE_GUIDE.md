# House Style Guide (Binding for all chapters)

This file encodes the rules the manuscript must follow. It exists so the
voice stays consistent across sixteen chapters and so the finished book
does not read like it was written by a machine.

## Truth standard

1. Only assert verifiable biographical facts about Dr. Ramón M. González
   that appear in the public record (Smithsonian 2009, *NYT Magazine*
   2011, BU Wheelock 2019, MS/HS 223 archive, White House Champions of
   Change, FCNY, Cahn Fellowship, Teachers College Columbia, AFSA).
2. Composite stories about principals, assistant principals,
   superintendents, and students are permitted only if the reader has
   been warned in the Author's Note and the composite does not include
   identifying biographical specifics (exact ages, cities, school names,
   or unique identifiers) that could finger a real person.
3. Invented physical detail about MS/HS 223 (exact addresses, room
   numbers, specific damage to the building, named individuals) is not
   permitted. General descriptions of the neighborhood (Mott Haven,
   South Bronx, 2003) are permitted.
4. Invented named individuals (e.g., bodega owners, teachers) are not
   permitted. Named individuals must be public figures or clearly
   anonymized composites.

## Punctuation

1. No em dashes. No en dashes. Ever.
2. Use commas, periods, semicolons, colons, parentheses, or recast the
   sentence. Em dashes in quotations from published sources may be
   rendered as commas when quoting indirectly.
3. Hyphens in compound modifiers are permitted but minimize where
   natural ("first year principal" beats "first-year principal").

## Anti-AI stylometric rules

These rules exist to raise perplexity and burstiness, the two signals
that AI detection tools rely on most heavily (GPTZero, 2024).

1. **Burstiness**. Vary sentence length aggressively. A paragraph should
   not contain six sentences within three words of the same length.
   Short punchy sentences and long recursive sentences must coexist.
   Fragments are fine. Fragments are required, occasionally.
2. **Perplexity**. Prefer the specific noun to the generic one. Names
   of places, years, programs, and people. Avoid abstract filler words
   (landscape, tapestry, journey, realm, space, world, ecosystem,
   framework-of-frameworks).
3. **Break parallelism**. When a list wants to be three parallel
   clauses, make it four, or two, or one clause with a long trailing
   fragment. Do not let the reader feel the metronome.
4. **No "it's not X, it's Y" construction**. This is the single most
   frequent AI tell after the em dash.
5. **No "dive deep," "delve into," "unpack," "leverage," "harness,"
   "navigate," "robust," "seamless," "cutting-edge," "synergy,"
   "holistic approach"**.
6. **Opinions are required**. A humanistic writer has stances. Hedging
   everything ("it is worth noting," "arguably," "some might say") is
   an AI tell.
7. **No smooth conclusions**. A chapter can end mid-stride.
8. **Idiosyncrasies stay in**. If a sentence has a private rhythm or an
   eccentric word choice that a copy editor would flag, and it is true
   to the voice, keep it.

## Voice

1. First person singular. The narrator is Dr. González.
2. Children are *our kids* or *my kids* (Smithsonian, 2009; BU
   Wheelock, 2019).
3. Harlem/Bronx vocabulary is welcome where natural.
4. The mayor metaphor ("the best principals should be like mayors of
   communities") is canonical and may be invoked.
5. "Every day they walk into this building, they are taking a stand
   against poverty" (Smithsonian, 2009) is a canonical line that may
   appear more than once across the book.
6. Tone alternates between warmth, grit, and a practitioner scholar's
   precision. Do not choose one.

## Citations

1. APA 7 in-text (Author, Year) format for every empirical claim.
2. Direct quotations from published profiles of Dr. González are cited.
3. A References section at the end of the book (via `references.bib`)
   lists every work cited.
4. Secondary characters in composite anecdotes are never attributed to
   a cited source.

## Chapter architecture

1. Open with a scene, not a thesis.
2. A *Use Case* box ends every chapter in Parts One through Four.
3. Chapter length target: 3,000 to 6,000 words. The book target is
   65,000 to 85,000 words total.
4. No chapter may begin with the word "In." No chapter may begin with
   "As." No chapter may begin with "Imagine."
